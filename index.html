Fly it

Zero steps between “I want that shot” and flying.

Palm takeoff: hold out your hand, press one button, and it lifts off. Gesture control takes it from there — raise a palm to hover, move your hand to steer, bring your palms together or apart to set the distance. You can even talk to it: voice control works through your phone or Bluetooth headphones. When you’re done, Return to Palm brings it back to land in your hand.

There is no controller to charge, no phone to clip on, no sticks to learn. And if you want them later, it also flies with the DJI Fly app, the RC-N3, or a motion controller with goggles — controller-free is the default, not the only way.

  • Palm takeoff and landing — no launch pad, no flat ground needed
  • Gesture + voice — fly it while your hands are busy
  • SelfieShot — pick a shot from the palm menu and it flies the pattern itself
Safety

The original Neo had no obstacle sensing. This one sees in every direction.

Monocular vision sensors face every way, backed by forward-facing LiDAR and downward infrared — with full propeller guards around all four rotors. ActiveTrack follows at up to 12 m/s in eight directions, including a Cycling follow mode that keeps up on the bike.

Sensing helps you avoid obstacles — it does not make the drone crash-proof. Fly it like it can still meet a branch, because it can.

  • Level 5 wind resistance (10.7 m/s) — steady in a breeze, but plan short shoots
Camera

A 12MP camera that knows its job: you, in motion, in vertical.

The 12MP 1/2-inch sensor (f/2.2) shoots 4K/60fps, 4K/100fps slow motion with a remote controller, and 2.7K vertical made for Reels and TikTok — steadied by a new 2-axis mechanical gimbal.

Honestly: this is not a low-light landscape camera, and highlights can blow out in harsh sun. It is built to keep you sharp while you move — and at that job, it delivers.

  • 49GB holds about 105 minutes of 4K/60 footage — no card to buy or lose, and no card to swap when it fills
The honest bit

19 minutes. No microSD slot. Plan around both.

Flight time is up to 19 minutes — 18 with the transceiver fitted — and real-world flying is shorter. That is fine for selfie shots; it is not a 40-minute session on one battery. The Fly More Combo exists for that.

Storage is the same trade: 49GB is built in and there is no card slot, so transfer your footage before you fly again. And one reviewer had Return to Home cancel in moderate wind — keep it in sight, and land by hand with Return to Palm when in doubt.

  • Wi-Fi range is 500m without the transceiver; the 10 km O4 range needs the Digital Transceiver fitted (+9g)
