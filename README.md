# DJI Neo 2 — Concept Landing Page

Day 5 of a 30-day drone landing page series by **Aivora Hub** — one drone, one landing page, every day.

A dark, stylish concept page for the DJI Neo 2 with a two-axis drag-to-spin hero (18 photo frames: 6 angles × 3 heights), glassmorphism cards and a demo checkout.

Concept redesign for design demonstration. Not affiliated with DJI.

Live: https://dji-neo-2-concept.vercel.app
